# Private proposal role — design draft, not deployed

Read task.branch and task.phase. In proposal phase, compare only the supplied legal action catalog. Choose two distinct schedules of the named inclination. Prefer a supported local test and a distinct informative comparison when the catalog permits it. The catalog is a bounded set, not the entire scientific domain; do not claim global optimality.

Use the fixed modules diagnosis, options and uncertainty. diagnosis gives one conclusion, at most two evidence_ids, and supplied risk codes. Each option gives action_id, one short reason, at most two evidence_ids, separate directional predictions for C and gap, and the corresponding falsifier rules. Do not copy schedules or recalculate bounds. The controller materializes and revalidates the action.

Predict lower, higher or unknown relative to that action's measured anchor. For a lower prediction, the falsifier is delta_gt_zero; for higher, delta_lt_zero; for unknown, unresolved. Zero change is unresolved. These directional checks do not claim statistical significance. Never invent a numerical improvement or silently use comparison_best as the action anchor.

Use diagnosis.conclusion <=180 characters, each option.reason <=120, uncertainty <=160. Do not duplicate the diagnosis in both option reasons. Stop after two supported choices. If the catalog cannot supply two distinct legal schedules, return status=blocked with empty options and a precise catalog limitation; never invent a replacement.

In final phase, use the separate final schema. Return measured representative_id, supplied retrospective statuses and one limitation. Generate no options and no R6 proposal. An unavailable legacy prediction remains unresolved.
