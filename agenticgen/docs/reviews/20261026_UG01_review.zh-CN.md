虽然首版没有相邻UG停滞触发，鉴于6pyy相对R0变化极小，本次主动检索并核查以下原始来源：
- [arXiv:2501.02526v1; NeurIPS 2024; equations 9-15](https://arxiv.org/html/2501.02526v1)：The clean-endpoint residual is differentiated with respect to the noisy input. Its conversion to a diffusion mean uses a model/time coefficient; transplanting the numerical diffusion scale to FLOWR is unjustified. This supports diagnosing the existing adapter, not changing it.
- [NeurIPS 2024 supplemental archive; SHA256 ffa7d8b0731b69defa9835bfebb089ad0200995b68270b34dd1442540f5321b3](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/file/faa6276ea12d7afeb3e42b210c86f688-Supplemental-Conference.zip)：Re-read cached official DiffSBDD/equivariant_diffusion/en_diffusion.py lines 602-624: detached condition target, autograd.grad(E_pocket,zt_pocket), and sigma2_t_given_s/alpha_t_given_s mean correction. The archive is evidence, not executable code imported into FLOWR; its sum reduction, feature guidance and mean projection differ from our frozen coordinate-only implementation.
- [arXiv:2502.02150v2, 2025-04-22; section 3.3, equation 6 and appendix A.11](https://arxiv.org/html/2502.02150v2)：Endpoint-Jacobian guidance has path-dependent coefficients and explicit distribution/path assumptions. It motivates distinguishing the VJP from the integration coefficient. The Gaussian-affine theory is not claimed as an exact posterior theorem for this molecular FLOWR stochastic implementation.
- [PyTorch 2.9 documentation, updated 2025-06-16](https://docs.pytorch.org/docs/2.9/notes/autograd.html)：Reverse automatic differentiation follows the recorded graph. Detaching the target leaves the endpoint-to-input derivative intact; frozen weights do not remove input derivatives. Correct local derivatives alone do not imply that a time-varying stochastic trajectory decreases final geometric error.

这支持对既有实现的解释与尺度搜索指引，不把扩散噪声系数照搬到FLOWR，也不把Gaussian-affine理论宣称为此模型的精确后验保证。
