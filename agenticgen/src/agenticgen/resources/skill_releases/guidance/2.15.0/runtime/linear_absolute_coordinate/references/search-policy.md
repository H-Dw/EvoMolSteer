# Search policy references

[Tree of Thoughts](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.10601) motivates alternatives and backtracking; [LATS](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v235/zhou24r.html) motivates feedback from completed trajectories. These mechanisms do not establish molecular benefit or justify additional model scoring. Here every node is a complete measured schedule; stage focus never resumes an intermediate molecule.

Increasing one Early window at two amplitudes gives one signed direction. Increasing two different Early windows gives different directions. Suffix-only adaptation of a historical prefix does not count as an Early perturbation. Reusing a complete measured schedule changes neither the population budget nor branch credit.

A newly adopted Late background triggers an Early revision in the next round. A pending obligation at the final budget boundary is recorded without adding a round. Intermediate predicted endpoints may rank two schedules differently from the terminal outcome; retain complete terminal evaluation.
