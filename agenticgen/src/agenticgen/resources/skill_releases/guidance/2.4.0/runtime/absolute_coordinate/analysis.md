The bound objective has three template-distance losses, each a half sum of squared pair-distance errors in Angstrom squared: condition_intra_loss, condition_outer_loss and interact_loss. Default binary weights are one, zero, zero; explicit interaction atom correspondences change the initial interaction weight to one. Distance means separation between atom identities, not absolute coordinate error, shrinking all distances, or an affinity score. The outer component compares every condition atom with every other template-mapped ligand atom, beyond the old two-bond neighborhood. Unmapped generated atoms have no template target and are not assigned invented distances. Interaction pairs compare each declared ligand atom to its specific fixed target atom in the shared template/generation frame. Empty interact_atoms means zero interaction loss. Pair-distance agreement alone does not guarantee chirality, chemistry, or a unique pose.

The primary recorded configuration ranking now uses condition coordinate root mean square deviation; all-pair root mean square deviation is diagnostic. When internal pairs do not exist, primary preservation is unmeasured, not perfect; the explicit executor fallback uses available intramolecular distances. Component weights are distinct from four temporal guidance scales: only component weights accept binary zero or one; a temporal normalized zero remains positive guidance. No length denominator or pair-count division is used in the reward. Inspect Angstrom distance errors, pair counts, raw versus applied increments and component losses together. More active pairs increase the summed loss and can change component balance. RMSD metrics still use means and remain distinct from the summed objective. Use calibration from this reward protocol only; historical mean/length-scaled strengths are not transferable.

## Protein-anchored absolute-coordinate contract

The fixed protein atoms alone determine one proper Kabsch measurement transform. Apply it to the predicted complex for loss and evaluation, never to the evolving sampling state. The ligand cannot influence the alignment. At the first active step, the executor selects one injective geometric assignment from the uncovered clean prediction using Hungarian matching, then freezes that candidate's assignment for the whole trajectory. Coordinate guidance and chemical state inpainting share it. Never rematch the terminal structure to improve the ranking, align the ligand alone, alter the protein anchors or infer atom identity from a small geometry error.

The single coordinate loss is one half of the mean squared condition-atom coordinate error, not a sum over atom-pair distances. The executor owns alignment, assignment, teacher-specific calibrated multiplier, complete schedules and the full coordinate vector-Jacobian product. There are no condition/outer/interaction component switches. Chemical channels and induced condition bonds are injected only into the state; coordinates, self-conditioning and the raw999 final prediction are never overwritten. Evaluate final chemical retention separately.

Native tool calls remain intermediate; final responses keep the supplied role JSON, full receipt identities and text length limits.

Actual guidance strength equals this teacher's frozen calibrated multiplier times two raised to its normalized scale. Zero is baseline, negative is weaker rather than repulsive, and disabling guidance uses a separate switch. Never average multipliers across teachers or alter empirical limits. Choose measured anchors, supplied temporal windows and signed integer changes from the legal action set; the executor constructs complete arrays and checks the normalized interval within [-10,10].
