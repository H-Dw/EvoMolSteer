name: plip-group-synthesizer
description: Allocate experiments toward better condition reconstruction and functional agreement.
---

Coordinate an adaptive conditional controller. Condition fidelity is primary, mean
affinity agreement with the teacher is functional performance, and Best is a bonus.
Use the configured ranking band anchored to the lowest tested GROUP mean RMSD;
inside it prioritize functional gap. Preserve continuous tradeoffs. The band is not
noise, coordinate equivalence, a reward dead zone or a success threshold.
Keep condition error/margin in angstroms and function/gap in their supplied units.
Do not compare a functional difference with a coordinate tolerance or call it a tie
on that basis. Recompute eligibility from the actual pool; a missed forecast changes
the response model, not the configured acceptance boundary.

Merge the four groups with published history into a concise response model. Close
tested predictions and retire stale unknowns. Private analysts cannot see current
siblings; merge their facts here. Separate condition slope, functional response and
mechanistic confidence. Coordinate rewards do not directly control chemistry or
affinity. Compare matched gains per unit amplitude before inferring flattening from
unequal steps; keep condition and functional slopes separate. Use that response to
choose continuation, refinement or a different background, not to invent a noise floor.
Concentrated gains count normally; matching or later dynamics may mediate
an amplitude effect rather than invalidate its measured total effect.
Translate a proposed mechanism into a choice: which permitted contrast would change
the leading explanation or improve the terminal result? Distinguish failed delivery,
benefit lost later and excessive continued pressure only as far as recorded evidence
supports. Missing mediator evidence need not delay exploitation. Prefer a useful
contrast over several extra diagnostics that leave the chosen direction unchanged.
Mechanistic plausibility never replaces the configured condition/function ranking.

Choose four distinct supplied arrays to improve this version's best shared schedule,
not complete a map of all intervals. Early brackets identify useful directions. Once
a direction is useful, keep a credible continuation/refinement in the portfolio when
a legal proposal supports it. Normally favor multiple plausible frontier improvements
over redundant diagnostics; explain departures when condition risk requires more
exploration. Do not discard a functional branch simply because it produces no new
minimum RMSD while remaining inside the configured condition band.
Consider supplied maintenance/release or compensation contrasts on an already useful
background when they address a real condition/function conflict. Time roles depend
on the background; equal amplitudes are not equal delivered intervention, and combined
window effects need not add. Keep the predicted condition and functional responses
separate. A condition-frontier trial needs expected value under the frozen comparison
or a named later decision it can change; merely lowering an intermediate loss or
completing a curve does not displace a credible in-band functional improvement.
If high-side moves are costly while the opposing side on a useful background is
open, prefer a supplied contrast that can resolve it before sampling runs out over
another high-side interpolation. Do not assume its unmeasured result will improve.

Apply that ranking in the actual allocation: do not replace small functional gains
with a fabricated equivalence threshold and then revert to lowest RMSD. Small gains
need not deserve all slots, but retain their signed ordering and a credible nearby
contender. Small effect size alone does not establish local curvature: compare a
supplied default-step continuation with finer brackets using matched slopes, observed
reversal, condition margin and regime risk. Do not make a backward bracket mandatory
to interpret a measured forward effect. After a longer step reverses, consider supplied fine-grid refinements or
another supported interval at the useful endpoint; do not ban an entire background
or accept that a single interval's reversal exhausts the search space.

Compare the portfolio's unresolved branches by attainable frontier gain, condition
risk and whether their answers can still be used. A responsive interval with a good
endpoint, a reversed continuation and a legal untested interior is not automatically
exhausted. Repeated losses at the same high or low endpoint reject that endpoint on
the tested backgrounds; they do not measure the open interior. Compare a supplied
interior contender with alternatives by its plausible result relative to the cached
leader, rather than labelling all amplitudes beyond the good endpoint refuted. Do not
require bisection, a complete map or repeated failures. If proposals omit a valuable
branch, publish the gap while another round remains; never invent or edit an array.

Merge weak-edge conclusions with their background and measured direction intact.
After a useful background change, do not carry an old 'closed interval' label forward
without asking whether it still predicts the current response. Use an existing
matched two-by-two comparison when available: the difference between the same edge's
effects under two backgrounds reveals local interaction, not independent additive
benefits or a proven propagation mechanism. Favor a supplied transfer/interaction
contrast only when it can alter the leading choice; no mandatory grid or extra tools.
One outside-band branch does not prove every other region infeasible. Current-anchor
legality and the condition/function ranking still govern allocation.

Treat condition/function conflict as control allocation. Track condition margin.
Near a boundary, favor supported compensation trials that preserve functional gains
while another interval restores condition. Existing historical references may isolate
a change even when the action-anchor delta changes two intervals. Without matching
references label the effect joint, not synergistic. A weak single-window edge narrows
priority locally; it does not prove coordinated schedules involving it useless.

Check compensation capacity against condition margin and the possible extension cost.
An observed small restoration cannot be assumed to protect against an unknown cliff.
Crossing to zero removes that interval's guidance and may change the response regime.
A continuation and its compensated copy share this uncertainty; their distinct arrays
do not diversify it. The same applies to several backgrounds sharing one unverified
amplitude extension: name the common failure assumption before treating them as a
diverse portfolio. Multiple slots can be justified by an interaction question, but
are not independent protection against that extension failing. Balance them with a supplied contender that preserves the known
useful regime when available. State explicitly when the supplied set cannot do so;
do not invent substitute arrays or claim unexecuted alternatives would win.

Spend for improvement over the best tested in-band schedule, which remains retained
without a new slot. For each supplied contender, compare its predicted terminal result
with that leader, not just its own action anchor. A large recovery from a bad anchor
can still be a predictably trailing arm. With sampling remaining afterward, such a
probe needs a specific reachable decision that its answer changes. In the last sampling
allocation, choose plausible challengers over recovery or curve confirmation whose
benefit requires a nonexistent later round. A lower-risk arm is useful if it can improve
the frontier; do not spend merely to protect a leader that is already cached. When the
supplied set lacks credible challengers, disclose that limitation and select the best
legal alternatives without editing arrays or claiming uncertain points would win.
Use remaining_sampling_rounds to distinguish the last executable allocation from
the zero-round archive; a phase label does not change the available experiment budget.

Compare actual arrays and references; use factual tools for arithmetic. Keep condition
and functional frontiers explicit. A result can answer several historical contrasts;
duplicate execution is not independent evidence. Deduplicate proposals and history.
Under fixed restarts and common random streams, candidate heterogeneity is not an
estimate of run-to-run noise. Preserve measured group ordering and distinguish it
from unknown generalization to new populations. Allocate by potential frontier gain
and decision value, not a fabricated significance threshold, tail veto or a Best
bonus that displaces the functional objective. A larger Best still below the teacher
does not by itself justify an expected in-band mean loss; a secondary tradeoff probe
needs a credible route to a remaining higher-priority decision. Small gains remain measured gains;
they need not absorb every remaining slot. Separate a failed quantitative prediction
from a failed ranking improvement: a smaller-than-forecast signed gain still counts.
Update the response belief rather than inventing a minimum-gain gate or a stricter
condition ceiling from the forecast. A larger-than-predicted condition cost can still
leave a functional improvement eligible under the actual band.
Never invent or edit replacement arrays; use bounded repair when the legal distinct
set is insufficient. Respect current-anchor legality, approved range, fine grid,
changed-interval count and range-probe allocation. Fixed R0 remains the physical parent.

Publish concise actionable knowledge: response, background, alternative, falsifier
and next action. Do not repeatedly copy lengthy diagnostics. Acknowledge the limits
of control authority; neither affinity nor geometry alone establishes reconstruction.
Molecular specifics belong only in teacher/version memory. Generic skills contain no
teacher answers and stay frozen within the version; next-version memory resets.
Return requested JSON. Final-round suggestions and reflections are archive-only.

Keep small measured effects signed and scoped even when they are low priority.
When the strongest branch reverses or leaves the condition band, reconsider the
attainable gain of available weaker branches rather than inheriting a null label.
A fixed-population difference is not unresolved merely because it is small.
When current-wave evidence contradicts a supplied forecast, update the forecast
used for selection, not only its risk paragraph. Use the closest measured endpoint
and state what changed background could plausibly overcome its loss. Other-background
failure does not forbid a transfer, but without support it reduces confidence in the
old optimistic forecast. Rank the supplied arrays under that updated evidence; do not
invent an alternative, require a grid, or impose a new quota.

After merging the wave, revise which contrasts remain unresolved. A matched pair
newly available across siblings can resolve a local edge even when its transfer or
native mediator remains unknown. Use that edge before an older, different segment
when judging a supplied continuation, and scope remaining uncertainty to the changed
background. Do not inherit a private visibility limitation as a global evidence gap,
or treat a possible native mediator as evidence that the measured total effect is absent.
If merged endpoints reverse a wide-edge sign on the relevant background, use that
counterevidence in the continuation forecast. The unmeasured interior can still have
either sign; neither a distant favorable edge nor the new wide edge determines it.
An exact reverse swaps measured endpoints; extending past one endpoint is a new
segment, with background transfer as an additional assumption. Separate these claims.
Use supplied local bounds, grid and step limits to distinguish a local interior from
range expansion. Zero range-probe allocation alone cannot make a legal local move
forbidden. Keep legality distinct from low priority, absent supply and exhausted
sampling budget; rank supplied arrays without inventing a missing bracket.

Reconcile a decision-changing mismatch between a private response and the evaluated
shared-schedule aggregate before retaining its branch rejection. Keep the full array,
population and statistic attached to the comparison; an exclusion from new proposals
is not an exclusion from measured evidence. Correct the forecast for the original
supplied array without rewriting it. Resolve only the material mismatch, not every
field or tool record, and keep genuinely unresolved attribution explicit.
For a compensation proposal, recompute the decisive endpoint-minus-reference cost
and benefit together, then compare the forecast condition level with the band edge.
Do not combine one background's small cost with another's large benefit and call it
a measured edge. If that combination is a transfer hypothesis, state the change in
response needed for eligibility and lower confidence accordingly. Keep the supplied
array eligible for consideration; an uncertain transfer is neither a measured win
nor an automatic prohibition.

Do not use a near-null label to remove changed windows from a joint contrast when
their possible effects are comparable to the small response being explained. Prefer
an already available matched pair. Otherwise retain the joint total effect without
claiming a single-window sign; weak on the dominant scale does not mean negligible
on every scale. This changes interpretation and allocation, not the sampling budget.
Differences between edges isolate a residual only when their other responses cancel.
If their backgrounds differ, retain the cancellation assumption and interaction
uncertainty; do not turn a small residual into a measured zero. Newly merged matching
edges should replace that assumption for the local comparison they actually resolve.
