---
name: plip-schedule-review
description: Review each teacher's best round once and improve terminal-control reasoning and exploration allocation.
---

Act as a training-strategy designer. Full-session review is prohibited. Read aggregate
performance for all rounds, select each teacher's best shared schedule by the frozen
condition/function ranking, and inspect only its winning private hypothesis/analysis
and that round's council strategy once, with relevant objective/action/budget context.
Do not resume a 52-session checklist, audit every tool call or read all failed contexts.
Keep failure archives/costs; investigate only an execution-blocking fault. Use recorded
concise rationales, not unrecorded private thoughts.

First identify what this controller can actually change. Four interval amplitudes
affect delivered coordinate corrections and subsequent native generation, not direct
graph or affinity commands. Literature supplies competing mechanisms, not a preferred
window, validated effect or extra action permission. Preserve the frozen metric:
GROUP mean terminal condition error, a pool-minimum-anchored band, then functional gap.
Do not import a joint-success threshold, extra baseline, new seeds, gradient route,
dynamic gate, masks or time windows from a broader research framework.

For each teacher, focus the assessment on three connected questions:

1. What explains the best observed terminal response? Trace requested amplitude,
   measured delivery when available, possible native propagation and terminal
   condition/function. Distinguish a delivery limitation, proxy-to-terminal mismatch,
   later erasure and excessive intervention as competing explanations. An immediate
   proxy gain, correct derivative, large residual or cap activity alone cannot decide.
   Reuse factual tools only for a calculation that changes the strategic conclusion;
   unavailable mechanism evidence is uncertainty, not a request for a broad audit.
2. Which permitted contrast would discriminate the important rival while still having
   improvement potential? Consider establishment, later maintenance, release and
   compensation on matched backgrounds. Do not impose early/middle/late roles by
   convention, add single-window gains as if independent, or treat equal nominal
   scale as equal delivered dose. Distinguish a schedule's measured total effect from
   an isolated timing mechanism. Use the existing full-run budget and action space.
3. Did the allocation exploit useful directions while its answers could still matter?
   Compare the selected rationales with all-round aggregate outcomes, without reading
   other full conversations. Favor resolving valuable reversals or functional branches
   over low-value curve completion. Several distinct proposals can share one failing
   assumption. A mechanism that remains uncertain does not cancel a measured gain;
   an unexecuted alternative has no performance result.

In the selected contexts, distinguish a useful local rejection from premature axis
closure. Aggregate schedules can show an unresolved interior or a background-dependent
response without reading more sessions. Use only the matched contrasts needed to
judge the next decision; absence of a test does not establish that it would win.
Check whether allocation compares with the retained best schedule or merely recovers
from a poor action anchor. Once no later sampling can use the answer, a predictably
trailing probe needs a credible chance to challenge the leader, not a safety label.
Distinguish falsifying a forecast from rejecting a real smaller ranking gain, and
rejecting a tested endpoint from declaring its untested interior infeasible. Preserve
private-agent visibility limits when judging what the council should have merged.
Check the decision meaning of thresholds: coordinate eligibility, functional order
and a response forecast are separate quantities with separate units. A forecast
failure must not silently become an extra acceptance gate. Ask whether a smaller
step or local-peak label follows measured curvature/risk, rather than small effect
size alone; distinct arrays may still share one unverified extension. Judge these
through decisions and outcomes, not a field-by-field wording audit.

Write a short assessment per teacher: the useful hypothesis, actual terminal tradeoff,
the main rival/limitation and a better next direction. Derive one to three generic
capability hypotheses across the review. Each needs expected decision behavior, a
legal discriminating result and a next-version falsifier. Rework existing guidance
instead of appending an error catalogue. Preserve demonstrated learning and productive
exploration; do not generalize one mistaken explanation to all agents. Teacher IDs,
molecular answers and optimal arrays remain in archives, never generic skills.

Use the next real version to judge behavioral and performance consequences separately.
Better phrasing or more diagnostics is not success; report condition/function, quality,
tail and cost under the frozen comparison. Development-teacher improvements do not
establish skill causality or generalization. If the supported amplitude space seems
insufficient, record the evidence and a future protocol-level question rather than
silently enabling a new mechanism. Range expansion needs actual boundary/delivery/
clipping evidence and the existing version-boundary authorization; neither low gain
nor clipping alone suffices. Two adjacent completed-version regressions still trigger
the best compatible historical strategy; Best alone does not.

After rollback, use the restored strategy as a supported base, not a permanent ban
on learning from the experience framework. Select only the modules that address an
observed decision weakness; retain, rewrite, combine or omit their wording as the
actual action space and results warrant. Prefer one or two compact changes over
replacing the whole skill or importing a research checklist. Record the base, chosen
module, empirical reason, expected action and next-version falsifier. Assess useful
terminal decisions, not whether the controller repeats the framework vocabulary.
Already launched versions remain frozen. Prepare additions for the next reviewed
boundary, and revise them after that predecessor's own limited review. Keep an exact
restoration receipt distinct from a later adapted skill; do not call edited skills
byte-identical restoration or silently bypass the existing rollback gate.

Preserve budgets, within-version freezing, range/rollback rules and explicit
best_round_strategy review binding with full_review_completed=false. Literature-based
skill preparation is not approval of a not-yet-finalized version. Verify finalization,
selected evidence and source/skill binding programmatically. Do not run local automated
tests or offline LLM replay. Review the diff, commit/push, and deploy at a reviewed
version boundary. Skills/document-only edits need no unit suite; necessary software
checks for executable changes run only in the fixed remote environment. Retain locks
and completed results, and report the limited review scope honestly.

In the selected contexts, separate a low-priority signed response from a zero effect.
After a dominant branch meets a measured boundary, assess whether weaker available
branches were reconsidered by attainable gain, without requiring a particular trial.
For the selected council, ask whether newly merged failures changed the forecast
used to rank supplied arms or only added cautionary prose. Preserve private visibility
and allow background transfer as an uncertain hypothesis; do not assert an untested
alternative would win or inspect other sessions to explain missing supply.

Judge uncertainty at the visibility level of the selected decision. A private agent
may correctly lack a sibling endpoint; after merging, the council may have a matched
local contrast. Check whether that contrast updates the response model while leaving
background transfer and native mediation appropriately uncertain. A mediator is not
an automatic alternative to a measured total effect. Use only decision-relevant
contrasts already in the selected materials and aggregates, without broader audit.
Distinguish exact endpoint reversal, an unmeasured next segment and background
transfer in the selected response model. A newly merged wide-edge reversal should
update a forecast without claiming the unmeasured interior is settled. If a branch
is called forbidden, compare that claim with the supplied local bounds and step
limits: range-probe quota, budget priority and missing supply are different reasons.
Do not infer earlier allocation causes or require a particular interior experiment.

For a decision-changing attribution mismatch, compare the selected rationale with
its shared-schedule aggregate and visible population semantics. Distinguish a private
misreading corrected by council from a wrong final ranking or an unproven software
fault; do not open successful tool traces to locate a speculative cause. Also check
whether a weak-window label hides changes large enough to confound the local edge
being explained. An available matched pair outranks that simplification; otherwise
retain the joint effect and unknown decomposition. Archive-only errors have no later
sampling loss, and untested alternatives have no result.
For a decisive compensation forecast, check that cost and benefit come from the same
complete edge before judging the claimed band margin; a mix of backgrounds is a
transfer assumption, not a measurement. For a residual obtained by subtracting edges,
ask whether background responses can actually cancel. Use the selected contexts and
existing aggregates only; do not widen review or demand new probes. Distinguish an
unsupported forecast from a wrong retained ranking and from archive-only speculation.
