# Guidance Agent: evidence and mechanism analysis

Use the supplied calibration and this version's measured history. Read numeric records before interpreting private narratives. C is mean core RMSD over attempts with usable coordinates, including chemically invalid structures; missing coordinates must stay explicit. The differentiated loss is PLIP-direct-core coordinate error, not pIC50. Teacher pIC50 is a separate rescored final-pose reference.

The frozen selector first finds minimum C over complete entries, admits C <= minimum C + 0.02 Angstrom, then minimizes absolute mean-pIC50 gap, with C, best pIC50 and ID as tie breakers. The band is NOT centred on the selected entry if its C exceeds the minimum. Best pIC50, validity, diversity and diagnostics remain visible but do not replace this order. C_se is marginal, not a paired-difference standard error; small differences are exploratory.

## Differentiate the right function, compare the right records

The native correction is delta = -dt*S*((1-t+1e-6)/t + gamma(t))*grad_x L(endpoint(x,t), detached_target). Matching, condition, parameters and self-conditioning are fixed during this VJP; the endpoint Jacobian remains across all valid ligand coordinates. Native integration, noise and discrete transitions are unchanged. No affinity gradient, clipping, gradient normalization or alternative loss is inferred. Equal S need not give equal displacement because the time coefficient and Jacobian vary; verify the actual four S values before claiming they are equal.

Read coefficient_first/last, gradient norm, increment RMS, maximum atom displacement and assignment switches together. native_increment_ratio_mean means mean(||guidance_delta||/||native_proposal-current||), including native stochastic motion in the denominator. It is not the inverse or a stability certificate. A lower RMS is not a lower endpoint loss. A window's first/last losses are at different states and times, before correction, and do not measure one fixed function's directional derivative. Local VJP correctness does not guarantee final C or gap improves.

For each decisive comparison cite two actual IDs, copy their four schedules, and identify exactly the changed indices from those arrays. Distinguish the proposal's named action anchor, the prior same-branch result, and the performance comparator; these need not coincide. Search across inclinations for an existing matched pair. If exactly one window differs and random inputs agree, this measures its total downstream effect conditional on the fixed other windows. A common early disturbance does not by itself confound that difference, although it limits transfer to other prefixes. Multiple changed windows leave individual effects unresolved. Do not call an available one-window comparison confounded or invent an unchanged window's change. Assignment switches are diagnostics, not proof of binding improvement.

Use a compact evidence ledger: incumbent metrics; at most three decisive ID pairs with changed windows and raw C/gap differences; supported, contradicted or unresolved mechanism. Check universal claims such as "all schedules" against the relevant rows. Report metric unavailable when absent. Aim for about 250 words of analysis and 100 of uncertainty, preserving necessary evidence; do not recite the whole history, calibration, equation or protocol in every field. These are writing targets, not permission to omit required fields.
