This is a self-contained extension of the verified endpoint profile. The primary
objective is matched-native mean predicted target affinity. Consider energy only
after a clear affinity gain; do not select a lower-affinity reward merely because
its strain is better. All failure denominators, score coverage and valid/unique
statistics remain visible. Discovery selection is adaptive, never heldout proof.

Analyst reads the recorded Steer selection support as one continuous window.
Use the independent-batch whole-window effects, confidence intervals, q values,
direction agreement, units and scale floors. Do not invent arbitrary time bins.
The sparse_coordinate_prior lists every field, including exclusions. Exclusions
reduce runtime reward terms; they do not delete failure controls or evidence.
Recorded scores are joint FLOWR latent-head labels accompanying endpoints, not
an independent geometry-only rescore. Coordinate correlations are hypotheses.

Prioritize coordinate shape, pair-distance kernels and receptor landmark fields.
Inspect whether the dose changes the actual path and whether positive/negative
candidate changes cancel. The controller normalizes gradients: multiplying the
entire reward does not usually increase its delivered dose. A flat response tests
eta/caps/gates; a negative affinity response revises target geometry or missing
features. A positive response below Steer retains a promising parent. Clear gain
permits secondary energy assessment among comparable-affinity programs.

Designer may extend the registered endpoint_direction formula with
geometry_feature_weights and geometry_direction_functions. A weight vector has
one finite nonnegative value per exact reference feature, with nonzero total;
nonzero values must come from selected fields in the supplied sparse prior.
Use binary weights or explicitly justify relative weights. Renormalize within
each geometric block so removing null fields does not accidentally shrink that
block's total force weight. Atom types, molecular graph equality and pair-distance
acceptance gates are prohibited selection controls.

The two registered direction modes are node_contrast (recorded stage high/low
contrasts divided by feature variance) and legendre_effect (recorded whole-window
effect curves divided by the stage-average scaled variance). In legendre_effect,
copy the supplied coefficients and their recorded score-time domain exactly;
never invent coefficients or treat d(feature)/dt as grad_coordinate. Constant
fits remain constant. The spatial force comes from differentiating the coordinate
observable whose coefficient may change with time. Stage trends need not be
monotonic; state where a supported coefficient changes sign. Temporal smoothing
is a small explicit function, not an additional fitted affinity network.

The pure geometry scalar is evaluated on the already required FLOWR endpoint
forecast. Its derivative is the actual FLOWR coordinate VJP at pre-native x_t,
with detached self-conditioning and fixed correspondence. Affinity outputs must
be detached and absent from loss. Production extra target/head forwards are zero;
one geometry backward per active step is permitted. The post-native injection is
lagged; without a new forward only its first-order response is estimated, not a
measured post-native endpoint reward change.

Read the dynamic window from evidence and cover every supported update with a
positive schedule unless its gradient vanishes. Finish native inference to1.

time_ramp_power is nonnegative. Full zero/native and actual-model finite-difference
checks must be reported honestly; their one-time extra forwards are separate from
