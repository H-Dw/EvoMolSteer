This is the active profile for the newly counted CK2 geometry campaign. It is
self-contained: do not inherit the historical motif-evolution balanced Pareto
selection or NOS/count-conditioned design preferences. Keep original Steer and
historical reports immutable. The maximum number of new inference rounds is 30.

Analyst must distinguish three questions: did coordinates receive a measurable
update, did the registered geometry reward respond, and did final predicted
affinity improve? Read actual injection, predictive-flow and stochastic-native
RMS, amplitude factors, caps/backtracking, active steps and continuation response.
Small affinity response warrants dose escalation as the first intervention, but
nonzero geometry/reward response with poor affinity also tests reward direction
and representation. Do not assert that nonzero guidance necessarily improves
affinity. Preserve unfavorable batches and failures.

Prioritize pure 3D coordinates: receptor-frame location, anisotropic second
moments, landmark distance/occupancy fields, full point-cloud correspondence,
spatial distribution of high versus low scored pre-selection candidates, and
their changes over the complete observed selection window. Atom types, count
strata and graph identity are not the discovery priority or acceptance criteria.
No affinity-head differentiation or additional per-step affinity inference is
permitted. Labels already recorded by original Steer may define offline targets.
Native FLOWR endpoint forecasts may condition coordinate correspondence without
extra network calls; freeze conditional assignments inside the coordinate
derivative and explicitly disclose that this is not a FLOWR endpoint Jacobian.

Designer uses registered differentiable coordinate formulas, not executable
free-form LLM code. Distinguish time derivatives from spatial gradients. Use the
actual score/proposal-state alignment and dynamic learned support, then finish
native inference to 1. All graph transitions remain free and no molecular SMC,
selection or cloning is introduced. Retain numerical derivative and zero/native
checks. Log effective displacement and response, not just a requested weight.

Reward PROGRAM selection is affinity-first. Report all/valid/unique predicted
means, best valid/PB-fast score, unique Top-5 and yield. The primary comparison is
matched-native mean; Steer mean and best are separately labeled benchmarks.
Use the campaign's stated meaningful-gain threshold. Before a meaningful head
gain, energy and shape are diagnostics, not reasons to choose a lower-affinity
program. After a gain, compare secondary energy/strain at comparable affinity;
never replace the primary objective with an unannounced balanced Pareto rule.
Basic chemical validity and missing-score coverage still remain visible.

For a flat result, propose a larger measured dose and record whether it escaped
the previous displacement regime. For a negative affinity result, inspect and
revise the coordinate target, correspondence or missing geometric features.
For a positive native contrast below Steer, retain the promising parent and
explore its dose, geometric sharpness and localization. Energy refinement comes
after the first clear affinity gain. These are decision branches, not proof of
the cause. New validation/heldout results assess frozen designs without retuning.

Verify skill behavior using actual candidate metrics and an independent Agent
response: the updated profile must change program selection from the historical
physical-first choice when the objectives conflict. Check the selected candidate
and proposed interventions numerically; echoing a skill hash is insufficient.
Save the exact skill/prompt/input hashes, literal response and behavioral audit.
Do this before inference, and repeat after a change to the objective or formula
registry. Keep compact evidence, assumptions, alternatives and decision ledgers.

Local edits and tests precede commit/push through the local VPN and exact remote
pull. Remote runs only inference/export. Verify prior reports before deleting
disposable generated data, and complete cleanup before the next inference.
