The primary objective is final paired mean predicted target affinity, with valid,
unique, best and Top5 outcomes reported separately. Preserve the measured global
winner when an experiment regresses. Energy is secondary after a clear affinity
gain. Historical Steer means and maxima are different benchmarks; a subset is
not the full experiment maximum and is not a paired heldout comparison.

Use the whole dynamic selection window. Coordinate shape, pair-distance fields,
receptor landmarks, raw endpoint point clouds and exact parent-child endpoint
changes have priority. Do not add arbitrary stage bins. Atom-type or graph-equality
acceptance controls are prohibited. Coordinate guidance may change chemistry
through native generation; retain every failure denominator.

Distinguish low delivered control from low final mean response. Inspect real
FLOWR finite differences, full zero/native parity, actual RMS dose, per-candidate
absolute affinity changes, signed batch changes and window-to-final response.
A flat result may justify a dose experiment; it does not prove zero control.
When control and window gains are substantial but final gains disappear, revise
the coordinate target or missing persistence features rather than simply raising
eta. R6 is such counterevidence. Its complete zero certificate is not a certificate
for a new loss. R7 is a measured global parent, not evidence every coordinate
attractor or feature mask will improve affinity.

fields. Polynomial fits contradicted actual nodes and are inadmissible. Copy
empirical functions and exact field names/weights. Whole-window q values do not
certify node-wise signs or causality. Salience uses absolute recorded effects,
not their time derivative as a spatial gradient.

The new lineage prior maps child forecasts to their actual selected parent before
computing geometric increments. Average children within each parent/score stratum,
then use independent batches for effects and confidence intervals. Its target
is a forecast-feature increment, not physical atom velocity. Only the fields
passing its recorded history criteria may carry the increment penalty. Currently
one of74 fields passes: landmark_00_softmin. Do not promote all24 coordinate
fields to statistically supported velocities. First-node increments are explicitly
unobserved placeholders and history is inactive there.

toward actual elite prototype feature vectors, with recorded elite score priors;
this has a finite maximum, unlike an unbounded high/low linear discriminant.
Keep alternative prototypes separate rather than averaging their coordinates.
Supported field weights are normalized within blocks; optional observed_abs_effect
salience weights noisy stages smoothly. This reward still omits many coordinate
details of R7's full point clouds, so it is an exploratory hypothesis.

An optional lineage term compares the current forecast feature increment from
its detached previous forecast to the recorded mean successful parent-edge
increment. It uses only the recorded history support, normalized to one unit
of history mass. Lambda controls its strength, with a continuous positive phase
power; it is a soft reward term, never a branch/graph veto. Matching increments
does not establish future affinity persistence or a causal region mechanism.
