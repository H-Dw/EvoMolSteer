---
name: agentic-steer-omissions
description: Audit information and implementation omissions that can bias pathway selection.
---

Inspect supplied definitions, field availability and the baseline selection
logic. Distinguish an absent field from an available but unused field. Check
whether preprocessing removes signed regional changes and whether extinction
or clones are incorrectly treated as outcome labels. Verify a claimed loss
against the actual metric: Euclidean distances distinguish opposite vectors;
taking feature magnitudes first may erase sign. Test a minimal counterexample
before diagnosing code. Distinguish panel construction from budget allocation.
Name the precise omission and its decision consequence. Select a contrast or
additional continuation that resolves it. A checklist without a changed
experimental choice is not evidence that the audit helped exploration.
