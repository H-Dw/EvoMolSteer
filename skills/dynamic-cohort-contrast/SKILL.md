name: dynamic-cohort-contrast
description: Audit adaptive quality cohorts and interpret spatial contrasts for coordinate reward experiments.
---

Use the supplied continuous scores and cohort policy to distinguish high quality,
observed lower quality, ambiguous boundaries and unobserved future outcomes.
An event-relative negative is not a failed molecule. Check how the threshold,
score separation, effective weights and ancestral-family coverage change across
the complete learned window. Do not turn missing futures into negative labels.

Compare positive versus remaining observed controls with spatially close lower
score controls. Match nuisance fields without matching away the regional fields
being investigated. Very close negatives can be mislabeled; check score margins
and ambiguity before increasing hardness. Preserve score distances as well as
ranks, and distinguish clone mass from independent generation batches.

Identify regions from measured spatial effects, uncertainty, multiple comparison
correction and cross-batch consistency. Explain the coordinate direction and its
time course, including counterevidence. A landmark shell field describes spatial
occupancy, not a proven chemical interaction or energy contribution.

For reward design, separate the evaluated incumbent from a proposed contrast.
Keep supported alternative regions and modes instead of averaging full molecule
coordinates into a single ideal structure. State which scalar is differentiated,
its scales and support, and which controls are experimental choices. Test one
change at a time and use final affinity outcomes to accept or reject the change.
