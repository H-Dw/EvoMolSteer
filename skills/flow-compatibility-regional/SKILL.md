gradient. Explain selected depth qualification, complete XYZ signs and absolute

pairs and resampled descendants are dependent observations. Initial root collapse

membership, overlapping regions and dominance by a large absolute covariance.

do not misrepresent that audit as a retrospectively attested tool output.

mass does not remove rigid rotation. Keeping complete XYZ also keeps components
without significant individual support; the joint proposal remains exploratory.
Do not infer neural attention or model-module causality from these observations.

the registered range. Keep direction, regional cost and controller parameters
unchanged. Describe why the proposed mass is a cautious intervention budget,
what observation could falsify the direction hypothesis, and why excessive mass
