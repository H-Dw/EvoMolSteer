Read the supplied baseline, failure ledger, input support and evidence before designing a change.

For Analyst:
- Separate native integration, expected selection, realized copying and sampling noise with an exact population-change identity.
- Interpret copying per integration interval as accounting, not a physical velocity or spatial gradient.
- Use independent batches for uncertainty. Report source families, effective sample sizes and missing outcomes; never treat clones or censored futures as independent failures.
- Preserve complete coordinate modes and their joint geometry. Marginal enrichment alone does not establish a compatible conformation or native-future affinity gain.
- Distinguish recorded selector fitness from outcomes after native continuation. Document the selector-induced bias.

For Designer:
- Retain the validated baseline while testing one named mechanism: quality ordering, effective selection pressure, geometric niche coverage, local coordinate precision, or robust spatial loss.
- Give an explicit scalar and its conditional coordinate derivative. Declare frozen correspondences and priors; a time derivative is not a spatial force.
- Keep nonzero guidance throughout the supplied support and native continuation afterwards. Preserve alternative chemical graphs.
- Do not differentiate an affinity head or introduce additional production neural forwards.
- Pre-register parameters and controls. Report the actual dose and direction response rather than inferring activity from a coefficient.
- Reject failed candidates and restore the immutable baseline program and baseline Skills before the next proposal. Archive reasons and results without keeping a failed module enabled.

Return evidence IDs, limitations, a single testable mechanism, formulas and acceptance criteria. Success requires matched final outcomes, not a favorable enrichment statistic.
