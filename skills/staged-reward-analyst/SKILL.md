description: Identify time-dependent coordinate targets and seed compatibility for smooth staged endpoint rewards on a supplied learning window.
---

Apply with the canonical Analyst and the supplied reward experiment contract.

Read actual coordinate contrast curves and available stage profiles across the
whole window. Examine changing regional direction, spread, covariance and
multimodal coverage. Distinguish stable within-lineage change from survivor
replacement; survival alone does not establish final affinity improvement.

Compare the supplied fixed endpoint forecast teachers with historical forecast
teachers observed at the matching score clock. Both concern endpoint forecasts;
an early noisy current-state cloud is a different observable. A contrast curve
does not define an absolute target. Do not average incompatible teacher clouds
into a fictitious molecule or claim neural-module causation from geometry alone.

Propose whether stage-dependent teacher relevance or geometric response scale
merits testing at fixed dose. Specify the observable, applicable seeds/region,
source support and falsifier. Changing effect sign is a diagnostic, not permission
to reverse an affinity objective. Keep gradual change when no reproducible knot
is supported. Do not assume that the best final teacher suits every early seed.
