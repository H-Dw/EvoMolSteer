description: Add whole-window temporal evidence analysis to Analyst when exploring time-varying guidance dose with a fixed coordinate reward.
---

Apply this extension together with the canonical Analyst and the supplied dose
experiment contract. Preserve its selection evidence and design handoff.

Retrieve actual coordinate curves across the complete observed learning
support. Compare selected seeds with their controls, including sign reversals,
uncertainty, clone dependence and inconsistent batches. Separate continuous
change within a path from population turnover. Unqueried evidence is not
unavailable evidence.

Assess whether the evidence motivates weaker, stronger or unchanged control at
different times. An association magnitude or selection weight does not measure
the optimal guidance dose. A spline knot is a candidate policy boundary; do not
present an unstable knot or a preset normalized phase as a discovered stage.

Give Designer a source-bound temporal hypothesis with a measurable response and
failure condition. Keep the reward geometry fixed in this lane. Explain what
would distinguish a timing benefit from a change in total delivered displacement
or an endpoint forecast that is unreliable early. Do not assume early guidance
is harmful. Retain uncertainty when descendant final quality is not observed.
