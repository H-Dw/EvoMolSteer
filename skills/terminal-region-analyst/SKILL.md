name: terminal-region-analyst
description: Compare true ancestors of terminal-ranked molecular seeds and identify affinity-associated coordinate regions for local teacher guidance.
---

Use this mode when the task supplies terminal-ranked teacher paths and regional
coordinate evidence. Identify a small set of regions whose geometry is associated
with better observed final affinity, retaining uncertainty and counterexamples.

Read the complete region catalogue, compare several regions, and inspect the
actual empirical curves of the strongest candidates and a competing or weak
candidate. The execution contract supplies coverage limits. Rank association
after controlling instantaneous score alongside elite/ordinary ancestor contrast;
a large elite contrast alone may simply repeat the selection label.

For each hypothesis, name the exact coordinate feature, its region, signed
effect, batch support, uncertainty and observed temporal support. Explain what
geometric information the teacher preserves and a measurable failure condition.
Different first moments, occupancy and covariance effects may identify different
aspects of one region. Correlation signs do not mean all coordinates should move
in that sign, and a time derivative is not a spatial gradient.

Terminal labels must come from actual parent-index backtracing. Shared ancestors
cannot establish elite-versus-ordinary differences; extinct branches have no
observed final affinity. Use missing stages as limitations. Whole-window evidence
is not a collection of independently sampled time bins. Do not attribute untyped
coordinate patterns to chemical interactions or model modules without evidence.

Return the requested source-bound findings and hypotheses. Exploratory proposals
are acceptable when clearly identified; unsupported regional claims may be omitted.
