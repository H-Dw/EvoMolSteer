name: terminal-region-designer
description: Select evidence-linked regions of terminal-ranked teacher point clouds for a constrained local FLOWR coordinate reward.
---

Use this mode to turn the actual Analyst handoff into the registered teacher-region
loss. Select a small subset of the regions the Analyst supported, and choose the
offered spatial radius using the observed geometry and its uncertainty. State the
expected local response and how a final affinity or quality failure would refute it.

The reward preserves intact teacher geometry in the chosen regions. Its compact
teacher membership has zero weight outside the selected support. It does not
average teachers into a synthetic molecular conformation. Retain alternatives
through the supplied mixture. The real FLOWR endpoint Jacobian can still couple
the resulting force to other current-state coordinates.

Tie every chosen region to an actual hypothesis and evidence source. Distinguish
an observed coordinate pattern from the hypothesized benefit of matching it.
Use an exploratory decision when the statistics are uncertain; defer if no
region is defensible. The interface fixes the temperature, dose, teacher labels,
derivative and window, so this task selects geometry rather than changing those
parameters. Evaluate final affinity first and report strain and validity separately.

Return only the supplied declarative schema. Changed atom/bond graphs remain
permitted. No affinity-head gradient, online rescoring or particle selection is
part of this reward.
